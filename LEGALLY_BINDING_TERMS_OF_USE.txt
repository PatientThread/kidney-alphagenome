MODIFICATIONS MADE TO OUTPUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Terms require notice of modifications. The predictions redistributed here
are not raw API responses. They were transformed as follows, and the code that
did so is in analysis/ :

  - For each variant, the predicted effect was taken for one target gene only,
    selected by matching the eQTL Catalogue molecular_trait_id, and averaged
    across the tracks belonging to a named GTEx tissue (analysis/09, analysis/12).
  - Values were joined to observed eQTL effect sizes from the EMBL-EBI eQTL
    Catalogue and reduced to rank correlations, sign agreement and bootstrap
    intervals (analysis/10, analysis/11, analysis/13).
  - No model weights or parameters are redistributed, and none were accessed.

Raw per-variant predictions as returned for the scored gene are retained in
results/ so that the transformations above can be checked.
